## Users

1. **Rental Customers (Penyewa Mobil)**:
   - Everyday individuals, tourists, and business clients looking to rent vehicles easily and transparently.
   - Context: Accessing on mobile or desktop, needing quick availability verification, vehicle spec comparison in IDR (Rp), friction-free booking, active rental tracking, and clear return instructions.
   - Job to be done: Find available cars for specific rental dates, book with required credentials (nama, alamat, nomor telepon, nomor SIM), track ongoing rentals, and return vehicles with calculated duration and costs.

2. **Rental Operations & Admin Staff (Pengelola / Admin)**:
   - Fleet managers and counter staff managing vehicle inventory, customer bookings, rental returns, maintenance, and registered users.
   - Context: Desktop/tablet office environment handling high-frequency operational workflows.
   - Job to be done: Manage fleet inventory (merek, model, nomor plat, tarif sewa per hari), verify customer credentials and bookings, process car returns with calculated durations and billing, and monitor operational performance.

## Product Purpose

Indrasari Rental Car is a modern, reliable car rental management and customer booking platform. It streamlines the end-to-end car rental lifecycle—from user registration and vehicle search to reservation scheduling, active rental management, return calculation, and fleet administration—delivering a seamless, transparent, and trustworthy rental experience tailored to the Indonesian market.

## Brand Personality

- **Keywords**: Professional, Trustworthy, Seamless
- **Voice & Tone**: Clear, dependable, hospitable, and modern. Direct operational clarity without bureaucratic friction.
- **Emotional Goals**: Instill confidence in vehicle availability and transparent pricing in IDR; eliminate anxiety around hidden fees, scheduling confusion, or return disputes.

## Anti-references

- **Cluttered Legacy Systems**: Dense, unstyled tabular data dumps, tiny crowded inputs, and confusing navigation without clear visual hierarchy.
- **Generic AI SaaS Slop**: Gratuitous gradient text, floating glassmorphism blurs, numbered eyebrow markers on every section, and repetitive card clones.
- **Friction-Heavy Checkouts**: Multi-step checkout mazes that demand redundant data or obscure the final rental calculation.
- **Disorienting Motion**: Over-animated interfaces that delay task completion or impair mobile performance.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity Over Complexity**: Present vehicle details, rental dates, daily rates (IDR/hari), and total costs upfront with zero ambiguity.
2. **Operational Efficiency**: Make high-frequency actions (search, book, verify, return, manage fleet) fast, intuitive, and error-tolerant.
3. **Contextual Localization**: Full Indonesian localization (Rupiah `Rp`, SIM validation, Indonesian phone and address conventions, Indonesian UI labels).
4. **Resilient Feedback & State Visibility**: Provide immediate, clear status feedback on vehicle availability, active rental duration, booking confirmations, and return calculations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG AA Compliance**: High contrast ratios (≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3:1 for large headers and key controls), legible typography, and clear focus states on all interactive elements.
- **Form Usability**: Clear labels, accessible validation error states, and descriptive helper text for required documentation (SIM, identity, phone).
- **Responsive Layout**: Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports for on-the-go customer bookings and desktop admin operations.
- **Theme Support**: Seamless Dark and Light theme support matching user preference and context.
- **Reduced Motion Support**: Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` settings for all state transitions and UI animations.
